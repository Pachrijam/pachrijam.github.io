## About Me

I'm a freshman at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute studying 
Information Technology and Web Science, with a dual major in 
Computer and Systems Engineering. I'm interested in building 
practical software and hardware solutions that connect thoughtful 
engineering with accessible, useful technology.

I started programming in high school with Python and Unity, then 
expanded into Java, C++, C#, JavaScript, SQL, HTML/CSS, and data 
focused Python libraries such as NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, and PyTorch. 
At RPI, I have continued that foundation through Data Structures and web development. 
My projects include a Monte Carlo simulation engine, 
a Gemini-powered Instagram caption generator, and this responsive portfolio website.

Leadership and community involvement are a major part of how I learn and work. 
As an Eagle Scout, I led volunteers in rebuilding a 60-foot boardwalk at the Fanwood Nature Center. 
I have also served as a marching band section captain for more than 30 musicians and am 
involved in Google Developers, competitive programming, and pep band.

Outside of software, I enjoy hiking, camping, visiting national parks, 
3D printing, gaming, and playing classical piano. My favorite pieces 
include works by Chopin, Liszt, and Rachmaninoff.
